Invisible at rest

Everything the iPhone island does, plus more.

Built from Apple's Dynamic Island and Android 16 Live Updates, with features neither has.

🫥

Invisible at rest

Shrinks to exactly your camera hole, with a calibrate mode for a pixel-perfect fit.

🎵

Now playing

Any music or video app: artwork colour, animated bars, seek progress, play/pause/skip.

💬

Preview, then decide

Tap to read the full notification in the island; quick reply inline (with ✨ suggested replies), or open the app.

📞

Calls

Answer or decline in the island (phone, WhatsApp, Telegram…), then a live call timer.

⏱

Timers & stopwatch

From your Clock app or the island's own tools.

🏏

Live updates

Navigation, deliveries, rides and live scores from any app (Android 16 Live Updates supported).

💳

Payments

Confirmation glimpse for Google Wallet and UPI apps (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, CRED).

⚡

System glimpses

Charging, low battery, silent/vibrate, DND, earbuds + battery, airplane mode, offline / back online, hotspot, NFC, unlock, flashlight.

🔴

Recording

Screen and voice recording with a red REC timer, camera/mic in-use dots.

🫧

Three at once

Several activities run together: one in the island, the next in a circle beside it with a +1 badge. Tap to switch, touch and hold to see them all.

🧰

Island tools

Long-press the empty island: 25+ tools you choose and arrange, any app, and shortcuts from your modules.

📥

Notification Inbox

Every notification kept (encrypted, on-device) until you clear it: search, filters, OTP copy, ✨ Catch me up.

🚫

No double pop-ups

Turn off system banners per app, or move notifications into the island; never pops twice.

🎨

Make it yours

7 themes, width, height, roundness, opacity, glow outline, animation speed.

📱

Simple app

A short first-run setup, then three tabs (Island, Modules, Settings) laid out the way iPhone settings are.

⏻

On/off switch

Turn the island off and on from the app or a Quick Settings tile; your Inbox keeps working.

♿

Accessible

Large-text ready, 48 dp touch targets, TalkBack labels, respects reduced motion and your "time to take action" setting.

🙈

Polite

Hides an app's activity while that app is open, hides message text on the lock screen, per-app on/off.

Add only what you need.

Modules are small add-on apps with their own permissions. Install one and its shortcuts appear in the island and on the app's Modules tab. The island itself never gets new permissions.

Install in two minutes

Works on Xiaomi / Redmi / POCO, Samsung, OnePlus, OPPO, realme, vivo, iQOO, Motorola, Nothing, Pixel and other Android 12+ phones.

  1. Download the APK from Releases and open it, or add it to Obtainium to install and update it straight from GitHub Releases. If Play Protect warns about an unknown developer, tap More details → Install anyway.
  2. Allow restricted settings (Android 13+): try the Notification-access toggle once, then go to App info → ⋮ → Allow restricted settings. 
    adb shell appops set com.ajit.dynamicisland ACCESS_RESTRICTED_SETTINGS allow
    No menu option? Use the command above from a computer.
  3. Turn on Notification access and the Accessibility service from the app's first screen.
  4. Keep it running: the app detects your brand and shows the right battery and autostart switches.
  5. Calibrate (optional): turn on Calibrate, then line the red circle up with your camera. Done.

Detailed per-brand guide: INSTALL.md

FAQ

Why isn't it on the Play Store?

Not yet. The project is new and open source. Install the signed APK from GitHub Releases or build it yourself.

Why does it need Accessibility?

It's the only way for a normal app to draw above the status bar and lock screen. The service listens only to "which window is on screen" events and can't read screen content.

Does it drain the battery?

It's event-driven: it reacts to notifications and system broadcasts, and animates only while something changes. Nothing polls in the background.

My phone has a notch or a corner camera.

It still works. Use the width/height and offset sliders to place it where you like.

It stops working after a while.

Your phone's battery saver is stopping it. Follow the brand steps in the app (or dontkillmyapp.com).
